# 🚀 REVENUEPILOT AI

### AI-Powered Revenue Intelligence & Growth Command Center

REVENUEPILOT AI is a modern business intelligence dashboard concept designed to help businesses identify revenue leaks, prioritize high-value opportunities, simulate growth scenarios, and convert business data into actionable decisions.

🌐 **Live Demo:**  
https://mk906036875-create.github.io/revenuepilot-ai/

---

## ✨ Overview

Businesses often have plenty of data but lack clear answers to important questions:

- Where are we losing revenue?
- Which opportunities should we prioritize?
- What could improve our conversion rate?
- What is our potential revenue?
- Which actions should management take first?

REVENUEPILOT AI brings these signals together into a single executive-style command center.

---

## 🎯 Core Features

### 📊 Revenue Intelligence
Monitor business performance and identify important revenue signals.

### ⚠️ Revenue Leak Detection
Highlight potential revenue leakage caused by:

- Missed enquiries
- Slow follow-ups
- Inactive opportunities
- Conversion gaps

### 🤖 AI Decision Engine
Generate priority recommendations based on business signals.

### 💎 Opportunity Intelligence
Identify high-value opportunities that may require immediate attention.

### 📈 Revenue Forecast
Visualize potential revenue trends through an executive dashboard.

### 🧮 Growth Simulator
Test different assumptions for:

- Monthly leads
- Customer value
- Conversion rate

Estimated revenue is calculated dynamically.

### ⚡ Executive Action Center
Convert complex business signals into a short list of practical actions.

### 🎯 Client Demo Mode
Use the interactive demo to demonstrate the product to potential customers.

### 📱 WhatsApp CTA
Potential customers can directly contact the business through WhatsApp.

### 📅 Book a Demo
Dedicated client-facing call-to-action for product demonstrations.
